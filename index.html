Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about immigration, visa applications, university admission, and consulting services.

Q1. What documents are required for a Student Visa application?

Typically, you will need a valid passport, offer letter or Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from an accredited institution, proof of financial capacity, academic transcripts, English proficiency test scores (if applicable), and medical health insurance.

Q2. Can I study in Australia without an IELTS score?

Yes, many Australian universities and institutions offer alternative pathways such as ELICOS (English Language Intensive Courses for Overseas Students), internal university language assessments, or accepting alternative test scores such as PTE or TOEFL.

Q3. How long does the visa processing take?

Visa processing times vary depending on the visa category, destination country, and applicant volume. Generally, student visa applications take between 2 to 6 weeks. We recommend applying well in advance of your course start date.

Q4. Am I allowed to work while studying on a Student Visa?

Most countries allow international students to work up to 20 to 48 hours per fortnight during official course sessions, and full-time during scheduled course breaks and holidays.

Q5. How does EduVista assist with university admissions?

Our expert education advisors provide end-to-end guidance including university selection, application preparation, document verification, scholarship guidance, and visa consultation.
