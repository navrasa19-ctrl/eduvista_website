Study in Malaysia

Complete Study Guide & Admissions Overview

Why Study in Malaysia? Global Foreign Branch Campuses & High-Value English Degrees

Malaysia is Southeast Asia's premier higher education gateway, offering foreign branch campuses of top UK and Australian universities, world-ranked public institutions (Universiti Malaya #60 globally), exceptionally low living expenses, and an English-speaking, multicultural environment.

Average Tuition Fees RM 15,000 – RM 40,000 / yr ~₹2.8 – ₹7.5 Lakhs / Year (1/3rd UK/Aus Cost)
Degree Duration UG: 3 – 4 Years | PG: 1 – 2 Years Twinning (2+1, 2+2) & Dual Degrees
Post-Study Work Visa Post-Study Employment Passes Fast-Track Multinational Hiring in KL
Work Rights While Studying Up to 20 Hrs / Week Permitted during semester breaks & holidays

🏛️ Foreign Branch Campuses, Top 100 Universities & Twinning Models

Malaysia hosts prestigious overseas branch campuses including the University of Nottingham (UK), Monash University (Australia), Curtin University, and University of Southampton. Students earn the identical degree certificate awarded by the parent institution in the UK or Australia while paying only one-third of the overseas tuition fees. Additionally, Malaysia's public universities rank among the world's finest, led by Universiti Malaya (UM ranked #60 globally), Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), and Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM).

💰 Low Tuition Costs, Economical Living & International Scholarships

Average tuition fees range from RM 15,000 to RM 40,000 per year (approx. ₹2.8 – ₹7.5 Lakhs) for undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Student living expenses in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor are remarkably low, averaging RM 1,200 to RM 2,000 per month (approx. ₹25,000 – ₹40,000), covering high-rise condominium housing with swimming pools, food, and transport. Scholarships such as the Malaysian International Scholarship (MIS) and university merit waivers provide substantial financial support.

💼 Regional MNC Tech Headquarters & Seamless Employment Pathways

Kuala Lumpur and Cyberjaya serve as the primary ASEAN regional headquarters for global multinationals including Shell, IBM, Google, HSBC, Intel, and Dyson. While part-time work during terms is limited to preserve academic focus, international students can work up to 20 hours per week during long semester breaks. Graduates in software engineering, finance, data analytics, and digital supply chains transition smoothly into Malaysian Employment Passes (EP) or regional corporate transfer programs across Southeast Asia.

🇮🇳 Exclusive Admissions & Visa Advisory for Indian Students

With an indigenous Malaysian-Indian population of over 2 million, Indian students experience an instant cultural homecoming in Malaysia, paired with prestigious British and Australian foreign branch campus degrees.

Academic Eligibility & English Waivers

Direct acceptance of CBSE, ICSE, and State Board 12th marks. 3-year Indian Bachelor's degrees are accepted directly for Master's programs. Over 90% of degrees are taught 100% in English with language test exemptions.

Indian Bank Loans & INR Budgeting

Extremely economical: Total annual cost is just ₹5 to ₹10 Lakhs per year (Tuition + Living combined). Readily funded by Indian education loans without heavy collateral.

Indian Community, Cuisine & Culture

Unrivaled cultural comfort: Tamil and Hindi are widely understood, Little India enclaves in Kuala Lumpur and Penang, hundreds of pure vegetarian South/North Indian eateries, and national public holidays for Diwali and Thaipusam.

Visa Formalities & Bilateral Accords

Smooth electronic Visa Approval Letter (eVAL) issued directly through EMGS within 2 to 3 weeks. Single Entry Visa (SEV) stamped at Malaysian consulates in Chennai, Mumbai, or New Delhi.

Key Intakes: February / March, July / August, and September / October
Language Exam: IELTS 5.5–6.0 / TOEFL 70+ (English MOI degrees widely available with language test exemptions)
Offer Turnaround: 2 to 3 Weeks via EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services)
UK & Australian Degrees at 1/3rd Cost

Graduate with an official degree from Monash University or University of Nottingham while studying in Malaysia and saving over 65% in fees.

Universiti Malaya Ranked #60 Globally

Study at world-class public research institutions with global reputations in Engineering, Business, Medicine, and Computer Science.

Ultra-Affordable Modern Living

Enjoy comfortable living costs of ₹25,000–₹40,000/month, including modern condominium housing, excellent healthcare, and tropical weather.

Effortless English-Speaking Society

English is the standard medium of instruction across universities and everyday urban life, ensuring a seamless cultural and academic transition.
